HOW THIS WAS BUILTvs freelance-ussr-map
Freelance map draws each SSR separately with grey + black stroke. This merges 84 polygons into one silhouette (no internal borders), then splits via clipPath + animating south polygon. Front is a geo LineString whose lat lerp moves south till 1 Oct (Erwin pocket shrinks). Daily simulation adds wobble, city flips, battle log, casualties, and audio from Google/Pixabay/MDN. War duration hardcoded 8 Sept → 1 Oct 2026 (23 days) — slider or autoplay to armistice.
